What happens next

  1. The editor will confirm the assignment within 2 working days.
  2. Once confirmed, the manuscript will be assigned to you through our submission system (JAMS). You will receive an e-mail notification from no-reply@jams.pub with instructions to access the full manuscript.
  3. The review is due within 10–14 days of receiving the full manuscript.

Please note: the editor makes the final choice of reviewers. If the manuscript already has enough reviewers, or another reviewer's expertise is a closer match, you may not receive this manuscript. We will keep your details for future manuscripts in your field.

Add no-reply@jams.pub to your contacts so the notification reaches your inbox and not your spam folder.
